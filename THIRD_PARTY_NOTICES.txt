LSWTSS Level Exporter 0.1.0 - third-party components

- BrickVault (TTGames DAT archive reader, C#): compiled into _internal/ArchiveReader/ArchiveReader.exe
  to read the user's installed game archives.
- Oodle (oo2core_8_win64.dll): NOT included. It is loaded at run time from the user's own
  LEGO Star Wars: The Skywalker Saga installation folder.
- .NET runtime (self-contained in ArchiveReader.exe), Microsoft, MIT licence.
- Python 3.12 (PSF licence), Pillow (MIT-CMU licence), NumPy (BSD licence), Tcl/Tk (BSD-style),
  packaged with PyInstaller (GPL with bootloader exception).

No game data is included. LEGO, Star Wars and The Skywalker Saga are trademarks of their
respective owners. This unofficial tool is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.
